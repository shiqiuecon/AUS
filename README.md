# AUS course materials

Course presentations by Shi Qiu.

ECO603 Applied Econometrics I is at [ECO603/](https://shiqiuecon.github.io/AUS/ECO603/): 14 teaching meetings, 404 narrated interactive slides, full teaching transcripts, 70 practice questions with worked explanations, and individual or combined slide PDF downloads. The instructor’s causal-graph framework is used throughout, with interactive conditioning choices and path tracing. Applied workshops use actual StataMP results and reproducible do-files with selected teaching variables. Every slide has a continuous locally generated English explanation with measured paragraph timing. Spoken explanations guide causal-graph conditioning and highlight the current point; selecting a paragraph replays it. Narration can be paused, sought or played at different speeds, and automatically pauses for practice. Audio is included in the self-contained offline lessons and is also available as a separate download. The player supports slide jumping, Fit/Zoom and panning, full screen, paragraph following, point highlights, saved practice progress and self-contained offline lessons. The sequence retains regression, time-series, panel, IV and binary-outcome coverage.

ECO351 narrated lessons are at [ECO351/](https://shiqiuecon.github.io/AUS/ECO351/?v=eco351-econometrics-v2): 26 classroom lectures and four supplementary decks, preserving the classroom graphs, equations, and raw Stata output. Expanded explanations focus on the course applications, interpretation, and causal assumptions. Locally generated narration is paired with a synchronized paragraph transcript; select a paragraph to replay its explanation. The course index links each tested lesson using its content revision. Speed controls, automatic advancement, and offline downloads are included. Unreleased Quiz 3 and Quiz 4 recap pages are withheld; assessments and instructor files are not published.

The QBA201 index links all 13 lecture decks covering Lectures 1–25. Each lecture folder holds a self-contained presentation with its narration, equations, and charts embedded. Combined lecture numbers follow the classroom decks. Add future narrated lectures in separate folders under `QBA201/` and add their links to the course index.

Click **Start narrated lesson** once to enable audio. Each slide has a separate recorded explanation. Playback advances after the narration and stops at the end. Pause, change speed, jump to a slide, or read its transcript at any time. The QBA201 recordings use a locally generated synthetic voice with expanded business explanations. The side script follows each spoken paragraph; select a paragraph to replay it. Playing the lessons does not call an audio-generation API.

The lesson pages preserve the refined classroom slides as vector graphics with LaTeX equations. On small screens, use **Zoom** and pan to enlarge slide content. Lectures 18–19 additionally include interactive confidence-interval illustrations. Each HTML file can also be downloaded and opened offline.

This repository and its GitHub Pages website are public. Anyone can view or copy the published materials.

GitHub Pages publishes **main → /(root)**. The root page links to the QBA201, ECO351 and ECO603 course indexes, which link to their available lectures. `.nojekyll` preserves folder names containing underscores.

Some course pages request `noindex, nofollow`. This does not restrict access. The lesson requires one click on **Start narrated lesson** to enable sound, then advances automatically and stops at the end.

Public teaching materials belong here. Formative practice may include worked solutions; original assessment papers, instructor answer keys, student records, and private authoring folders are not part of this repository.
