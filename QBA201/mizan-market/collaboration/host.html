      Winning-team bonus · +1 course percentage point per verified member

      Score six common criteria from 0 to 2. Tied top teams are joint winners, with the full bonus for each verified member. Close all checkpoints, verify rosters and review each eligible team before finalizing.

      How to run this
      1. Add teams and privately give each team leader their access code or link.
      2. Team leaders enter members’ names and IDs. Open the matching checkpoint when the class reaches it.
      3. Close each checkpoint at the deadline. Team leaders can revise while it is open, and every saved version is recorded.
      4. After all checkpoints close, review each team’s answers, verify attendance and roster, and score the rubric.
      5. Finalize the bonus record, then save the bonus CSV and full answer record.

      The records remain accessible for 14 days, unless you clear the session. The downloaded files preserve them for grading.
