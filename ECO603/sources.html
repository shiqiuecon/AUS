Sources and reproducibility

The course is being redesigned by Shi Qiu at the American University of Sharjah. The review edition draws on the Spring 2026 ECO603 syllabus, lectures, homework and examination skills, and the accessible teaching progression used in ECO351. This edition contains two draft lessons and a proposed whole-course sequence.

Observed data and genuine software output

Meeting 01 uses the 420-district California school dataset supplied with the earlier course, also used in Stock and Watson's econometrics resources. Meeting 11 uses the 108-month California accident dataset from ECO603 Homework 3, January 1981–December 1989 (the Wooldridge accident teaching dataset). Plotted columns are exported from these archived files. They are historical teaching data, not current policy evidence.

Stock–Watson student resources · Stata regress manual · Stata newey manual

Both examples were run in StataMP on 7 November 2026. Numerical output is extracted from the resulting text logs; it is not invented or reconstructed as simulated Stata output. Downloadable copies omit machine-specific paths. Full numerical results remain unchanged. Every simulation or small invented dataset in the lessons is explicitly identified as illustrative.

Reproduce the two examples

Save a do-file and its matching CSV in the same local folder, then run the do-file in Stata from that folder. The CSVs contain the variables needed for these examples, exported from the archived datasets. No user-written Stata package is required. The original binary datasets and authoring records are not included.

What the graphics do

School scatterplots, residual graphs and fitted lines use the exported observations and fitted coefficients. The SSE slider computes loss on all 420 observations. The adjusted line fixes English-learner share at its sample mean. Traffic paths and lag plots use observed outcomes or residuals from the specified regression. The AR(1) control is a fixed-innovation simulation with a burn-in, not an estimated model. The HAC slider displays Bartlett taper weights, not recalculated HAC estimates. Interval graphs use the explicit large-sample 1.96 rule; exact Stata intervals remain in the output.
