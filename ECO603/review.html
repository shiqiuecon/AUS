Review lists

Use each item as a task you can explain, calculate and interpret.

Before the midterm: regression foundations

  • Define the observation and target; trace directed and backdoor paths and explain collider conditioning. Lesson 01 · Practice
  • Predict and calculate omitted-variable bias; justify controls and a common sample. Lesson 02 · Practice
  • Explain OLS, residualisation and the difference between errors and residuals. Lesson 03 · Practice
  • Interpret intervals and tests; explain BP/White diagnostics, robust inference and WLS/GLS. Lesson 04 · Practice
  • Translate level, log and quadratic coefficients into meaningful changes. Lesson 05 · Practice
  • Distinguish unbiasedness, consistency and precision; audit imperfect data. Lesson 06 · Practice
  • Calculate group slopes and joint restrictions; compare OLS, ridge and lasso using training-only validation. Lesson 07 · Practice

Before the final: comprehensive review

  • Declare dates, index bases and nominal/real units; distinguish trend, seasonality and exogeneity. Lesson 09 · Practice
  • Calculate impact and cumulative responses; explain persistence and stationarity. Lesson 10 · Practice
  • Choose conventional, robust or HAC inference for a stated dependence structure. Lesson 11 · Practice
  • Distinguish dynamic specification, covariance correction and shock responses. Lesson 12 · Practice
  • Explain within-unit variation, fixed effects and a real-data DiD; defend the untreated trend and inference unit. Lesson 13 · Practice
  • Trace compatible controlled IV stages; defend validity and explain weak-robust tests and exclusion sensitivity. Lesson 14 · Practice
  • Compare LPM, logit and probit probabilities and marginal effects. Lesson 15 · Practice

For the comprehensive review, revisit the regression foundations as well. Finish by writing a short empirical memo that connects a question, a credible comparison, a model, a magnitude with uncertainty and a material limitation.
