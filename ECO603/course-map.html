The step beyond ECO351

Keep the accessible progression, while asking students to defend identifying variation, distinguish unbiasedness from consistency, and explain time-series and panel inference. Matrix algebra is not a required entry point.

Old grading weights, dates and final format are not carried forward as newly approved policy. The new official syllabus determines assessment details.
